## Where AdsPower genuinely wins

- **No-code RPA depth.** Its visual automation builder is more approachable than an API for non-technical operators, and it's been refined for years around marketplace-seller tasks.
- **Firefox kernel option.** Some workflows specifically need a Firefox environment; AdsPower offers one.
- **E-commerce community.** A long history with Amazon/eBay/etsy-style sellers means most common workflows have existing templates and tutorials.

## Where RoxyBrowser wins

- **The free plan doesn't expire.** AdsPower's free tier is real but narrower; RoxyBrowser's 2 permanent profiles are enough to run actual accounts while you evaluate.
- **Published audits.** SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022 — independent verification matters when the tool holds your business's logins.
- **The AI-agent layer.** Automation beyond canned RPA: describe workflows and let the API/AI layer handle them — a different generation of approach.
- **Window Sync for human-paced batches.** When you want to act across 20 open profiles yourself — not hand it to robots — synchronizer is faster to use than any RPA builder.
- **Team Space.** Role-based profile sharing for VAs and staff, without credential handovers.

## The decision test that actually works

Write down your 5 most repeated multi-account tasks. Then check:

- Are most of them **platform-facing actions** (posting, listing, engaging)? → AdsPower's RPA templates will eat them.
- Are most of them **monitoring, setup, and management** (settings sweeps, session checks, environment juggling)? → RoxyBrowser's Window Sync + Hub is the better home.
- Mixed? Start on [RoxyBrowser's free plan](https://mrait.ca/go/freebrowser) — automation can be added later, but a permanent free base is the cheaper starting point.

## Who picks which

- **Marketplace seller running bulk listing operations:** AdsPower.
- **Agency juggling client accounts with some automation:** RoxyBrowser.
- **Security-sensitive operation (audits required):** RoxyBrowser.
- **Non-technical operator who wants drag-and-drop robots:** AdsPower.
