## Where GoLogin genuinely wins

- **Web access.** Managing profiles from a borrowed machine, a locked-down work laptop, or an iPad is GoLogin's home turf. RoxyBrowser is a desktop app; if installing software is impossible in your situation, that settles it.
- **Brand maturity.** GoLogin has broader name recognition and a longer public track record — if your procurement process weights "years in market," it scores.

## Where RoxyBrowser wins

- **The free plan is real.** GoLogin's free tier exists but is narrower; RoxyBrowser's 2 permanent profiles with no card let you run real accounts indefinitely.
- **Audited security.** SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 are published, independently examined credentials — the meaningful trust signal when the tool holds your business logins.
- **Scale workflow.** Window Sync (one action across many open profiles) plus the AI-agent layer plus role-based team sharing is a more complete package for growing operations.
- **Kernel freshness.** Chromium synced with official Google releases, so profiles look like current browsers.
