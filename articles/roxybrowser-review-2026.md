## What is RoxyBrowser?

RoxyBrowser is a desktop antidetect browser: it runs many isolated browser profiles, each with its own fingerprint, cookies, storage, and (optionally) its own proxy IP. It's built on Chromium and keeps its kernel synced with official Google releases, so profiles behave like current, ordinary browsers rather than an outdated build that sophisticated sites can spot.

It's operated by LINKV TECH PTE. LTD. (Singapore) and is used for the usual multi-account workloads: client social media management, marketplace stores, ad account management, affiliate operations, and per-identity workspace separation.

## Features, one by one

### Fingerprint engine

Every profile gets its own fingerprint — user agent, screen parameters, Canvas/WebGL signatures, time zone, language, fonts, and more. The important part is **consistency**: RoxyBrowser lets you customize fingerprint parameters to mimic real environments and holds them stable across sessions, so a profile doesn't leak "I'm not the browser I claim to be" signals between visits.

### Kernel freshness

Because the Chromium kernel tracks official Google releases, your profiles look like people running a current browser. An outdated kernel is one of the most common tells among antidetect browsers, so this matters more than most buyers realize.

### Account Hub

The dashboard view of all your profiles — searchable, groupable by client or project. Past ~10 profiles, this becomes the part of the product you live in.

### Window Sync

Open several profiles and perform the same action across all of them at once. For repetitive publishing, checking, or monitoring routines across many accounts, this is the single biggest time-saver in the feature set.

### API Flow + AI Agent

RoxyBrowser exposes an API and an AI-agent layer, so multi-account workflows can be scripted rather than clicked. If your operation is automation-heavy, compare this surface carefully against AdsPower's RPA — it's RoxyBrowser's most direct answer to it.

### Team Space

Share specific profiles with teammates or VAs under role permissions — without handing over the actual logins. For agencies, this is the difference between "manageable" and "security nightmare" at scale.

### RoxyIP proxy integration

A built-in high-anonymity proxy pool, plus support for bringing your own proxies. One IP per profile is the clean setup, and having the option integrated saves the separate vendor hunt.
